# Marry Me Chicken Gnocchi Soup

This recipe makes a hearty 6 portions (or 4 very large ones).

## Ingredients

**Roasted chicken**
- 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 3–4 medium)  
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil  
- 1–2 tsp Old Bay seasoning  
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning  
- 1 tsp paprika  
- 1 tsp garlic powder  
- Salt and pepper to taste  

**Soup base**
- 2 tbsp oil (olive oil or oil from the sundried tomatoes)  
- 2 medium white onions, diced  
- 1 large carrot, diced  
- 2–3 celery stalks, diced  
- 6–8 garlic cloves, sliced or minced  
- 1–2 tbsp tomato paste  
- 4–8 sundried tomato halves, minced (about 1/4 cup packed)  
- 6 cups chicken stock/broth  
- 1 cup heavy cream  
- 1 package gnocchi (16–18 oz shelf‑stable or refrigerated)  
- 1 packed bunch spinach, roughly chopped (about 3–4 cups)  
- 1/2–1 cup freshly shredded cheddar cheese  
- 1/2–1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese (plus extra for serving)  
- Salt, black pepper, and extra Italian seasoning or red pepper flakes to taste  

## Instructions

1. **Roast the chicken**  
   - Heat oven to 400°F / 200°C. Place a rack over a rimmed baking sheet and lay the chicken breasts on the rack.  
   - Drizzle with olive oil, then season generously with Old Bay, Italian seasoning, paprika, garlic powder, plus salt and pepper.  
   - Roast 20–25 minutes, or until the thickest part of each breast reaches 165°F / 74°C. Let rest, then cube or shred the chicken.

2. **Start the soup base**  
   - In a large Dutch oven, heat the oil over medium heat. Add the onions and cook until softened and translucent.  
   - Stir in the carrot and celery, season lightly with salt and pepper, and cook until the vegetables begin to soften.

3. **Build flavor with aromatics and tomatoes**  
   - Add the garlic and cook just until fragrant.  
   - Stir in the tomato paste and cook 1–2 minutes, letting it darken slightly, then add the minced sundried tomatoes and mix well.

4. **Add stock and simmer**  
   - Pour in the chicken stock, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.  
   - Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook 10–15 minutes so the flavors meld.

5. **Make it creamy and cook the gnocchi**  
   - Stir in the heavy cream, then add the gnocchi.  
   - Simmer, stirring occasionally, until the gnocchi are tender and floating, about 3–5 minutes.[3]

6. **Add chicken and cheeses**  
   - Add the roasted chicken pieces to the pot and return to a gentle simmer.  
   - Stir in the Parmesan and cheddar until fully melted and the soup is rich and creamy. Adjust thickness with a splash more stock or cream if needed.[1]

7. **Finish with spinach and seasoning**  
   - Stir in the chopped spinach and cook just until wilted.  
   - Taste and adjust with more salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, and optional red pepper flakes.

8. **Serve**  
   - Ladle into bowls and finish with extra Parmesan, a drizzle of olive oil, and fresh cracked pepper.  
   - This quantity comfortably serves about 6 people as a main course, aligning with similar gnocchi soup recipes that use 1 lb gnocchi, 6 cups stock, and 1 cup cream for 4–6 portions.[4][1]
